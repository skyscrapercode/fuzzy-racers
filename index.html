How to Play

Controls

  • ▲ Accelerate forward
  • ▼ Brake while moving forward: keep holding once stopped to reverse out of walls (capped at ~40% of top speed)
  • ◀ ▶ Steer left / right (works in reverse too)
  • Space Use held powerup
  • P / Esc Open the pause menu (Resume or Restart)
  • F Toggle the AI Fuzzy Brain inspector

If the AI gets wedged against a wall it will auto-reverse for a moment to free itself, just like you can.

Powerups

  • 🔴 Speed Boost +50% speed for 5s. Best used on straightaways.
  • 🛡️ Shield Immune to all damage for 6s; blocks missiles, oil, and rams.
  • 🎯 Missile Homing rocket at the car ahead. 15 HP damage per hit.
  • 🌊 Oil Slick Drips behind you; cars that hit it spin out.
  • ⚡ EMP Blast Stuns nearby cars for 2s.
  • 🔧 Repair Kit Instantly restores 40 HP.
  • 🌀 Tornado Spins out the car ahead.
  • 💨 Nitro Surge Massive 2s burst. Requires the Nitro Engine.

Fuzzy AI Difficulty

Opponents use a fuzzy logic controller that weighs distance to the player, gap, corner sharpness, health, and powerup readiness. Difficulty tunes how often the engine re-ticks, how much input noise it adds, and how strongly it rubber-bands.

  • 🟢 Rookie Slow 1.5s reactions, ±20% input noise, rarely uses powerups, mild catch-up. Prone to mistakes.
  • 🟡 Racer Moderate 0.8s reactions, ±10% noise, strategic powerup use, moderate rubber-banding.
  • 🔴 Champion Fast 0.3s reactions, ±3% noise, aggressive optimal powerups, strong rubber-banding, and ★ predicts your powerups.

Garage & Customization

Pick a chassis in the Garage, then tune top speed, acceleration, handling, and armor. Stats are budgeted: bumping one trims another. Paint colors are pure flex.
